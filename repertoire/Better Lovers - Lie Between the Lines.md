## The String-Skipping Problem

The clean intro has Guitar 1 skipping between the low D string (open) and the higher B/e strings for the melody. On all-downpicking, the pick tends to clip the middle string (G) on the way through because the wrist stays low after hitting the low string.

The fix is training the picking arc — the wrist needs to lift slightly after the low string hit so the pick clears the G string cleanly before landing on the target.

## Guitar Routine — Clean Intro (~45 min)

**Reference the HTML viewer in Safari for the score while working through this.**

### Warm-Up (5 min)
Slow chromatic runs across all 6 strings. Even pick attack, no rushing.

### Step 1 — Isolate the Skip (5 min)
Find each string-skip moment in the intro (any jump from low D over to B or e string). Play just those two notes in isolation — skip string, then target — slowly and deliberately.
- No metronome yet
- Focus: pick lands clean on the target string, no G string noise
- Once clean: add click at 50 BPM, 8th notes

### Step 2 — Two-Bar Chunks (15 min)
Break the intro into 2-bar segments. Work each:
1. **60 BPM** — clean 4x in a row before moving up
2. **75 BPM** — same standard
3. **99 BPM** — target tempo

Don't chain segments until each is solid individually.

### Step 3 — Picking Arc Drill (5 min)
When the skip keeps clipping at speed:
- Rest picking hand pinky lightly on the body as a reference anchor
- Play D → skip → target string slowly, watching the wrist arc
- The arc should feel identical every rep — burn in the muscle memory
- If you hear the G string: wrist staying too flat — lift it slightly after the low D hit

### Step 4 — Full Intro Run (10 min)
Measures 1–16 at 99 BPM straight through. Note any bar that breaks. Return to Step 2 for just that bar, then re-run the full intro.

### Step 5 — Push Into Section B (5 min)
If Section A is solid: look at the main riff (♩=135, page 8 of the score). Distortion, palm mute on open D. Don't aim for clean — just feel how it follows the intro.

### Cool-Down (5 min)
Free noodling in Drop D. No structure. Let the hands relax.
