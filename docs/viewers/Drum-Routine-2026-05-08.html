09Reed Syncopation — Snare Only 5 min
Progressive Steps to Syncopation · Reed · p.10 · Fresh page, snare only
  • Snare and hi-hat foot only — no kit orchestration, no bass drum
  • Read the full line before playing it — scan rests, note where ties cross the barline
  • 65 BPM 8th pulse — read every rest as if it were a note
  • After 3 clean repetitions of a line: add the bass drum on every quarter note
  • Do NOT orchestrate around the kit until those two layers are solid
Rule: The moment you reach for a tom, you've skipped a step. Snare + bass drum must feel locked before anything moves around the kit.
Progressive Steps to Syncopation — p.10
10Burnout: Kick + Snare Unison 5 min
No click · No structure · Feet and snare hit on every note together
  • Every note: both feet AND the snare hit simultaneously — kick, left kick, and snare as one sound
  • Start at a tempo that feels medium-fast — every hit is unison, no independence required
  • Build speed each minute until the unison starts to split — stop when it separates
  • Hands can add anything on top: ride, crashes, nothing. Doesn't matter. The unison is the thing.
  • Different from last session's sextuplet sprint and the straight 16th endurance before that
