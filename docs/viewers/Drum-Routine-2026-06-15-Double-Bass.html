Block 3 · 6 min — Left Foot Isolation
Donati Ch.2 — Left Foot Technique pp.28–30
Cold-start flutter — pre-activate before launching
80–110 BPM
  • Before each exercise: light taps at tempo for 4 beats with the left foot before the pattern starts. Don't cold-launch.
  • If strokes 2–3 flutter after entering: hard stop, RLRL × 4, re-enter. Don't play through the flutter.
  • Goal: entry is clean from note 1, not just note 4 once the foot warms up.

Block 4 · 5 min — Burnout
Straight 16th Endurance
  • Both feet alternating 16ths — RLRL continuous. Simple hand groove on top (ride + snare 2&4).
  • Start at whatever ceiling you hit in block 2. Hold until form breaks.
  • Rest 30 sec. Go again harder. 3 rounds.
  • No click on round 3 — push to absolute max, hold as long as you can.
