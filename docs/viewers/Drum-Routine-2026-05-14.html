3 — Latham: Funk Patterns 10 min
Advanced Funk Studies · Latham · p.22
  • Read through before playing — Latham grooves have a specific feel that needs to be heard first
  • No click to start — lock into the pocket before adding tempo
  • Ghost notes are the difference between a funk groove and a rock groove — keep them quiet but present
  • Add click once the feel is there, push to the edge of comfortable
Mindset: Feel first, accuracy second. A stiff groove played perfectly is still stiff.
