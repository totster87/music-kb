Block 7 · 5 min
Burnout — Kick + Snare Unison
  • Feet and snare hit together on every note. Simple hand pattern on top (ride or hat).
  • Start at a medium tempo. Add 10 BPM each minute.
  • Keep going until the form collapses — that's the end.
  • No click. No structure. Just push.
