Block 2 · 8 min — Technique
Legato Drill — Triplets / Eighths
No score · fix string noise from sixteenth-note pull-offs
60–90 BPM
Clean
  • Sixteenth-note pull-offs cause string noise — switch to triplets (1-trip-let) or straight eighths. The slower subdivision gives left fingers time to clear before the next string.
  • Pattern: 4-3-2-1 descending on each string, then shift position. Triplets feel: ONE-two-three, ONE-two-three.
  • Left fingers must lift vertically off the string after each pull-off — fingers staying flat clip adjacent strings. Think up, not sideways.
  • Volume across the 4321 group should be even — if 4 and 3 are louder than 2 and 1, slow down and even out the attack before adding tempo.
