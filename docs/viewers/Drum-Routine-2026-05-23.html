Block 2 · 12 min
PI Rock S1 — Snare/Bass Combinations BOTTLENECK
p.9 · exercises 1–4 · last worked 2026-05-04
60–75 BPM
  • Known problem: 16th-note alternating hats lose control when right hand crosses to snare — especially groups of 3 in measure 2.
  • Isolate just those bars. Play measure 1 clean, stop, then play measure 2 alone. Don't chain until each is stable.
  • If the hats are scrambling: slow to 50 BPM and rebuild the pattern in silence first (no sound, just motion).
  • Don't rush past 75 — this is a coordination problem, not a tempo problem.

Block 6 · 5 min
Burnout — Doubletime Bop
  • Pick a comfortable swing tempo (80–100 BPM). Lock in the ride feel.
  • Double it. Hold the ride pattern as long as you can at the doubled tempo.
  • When it starts to blur, back off briefly then push again. 5 min, no click.
