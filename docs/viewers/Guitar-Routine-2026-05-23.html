Block 1 · 10 min — Warm-Up
Rick Stone — Melodic Minor Scales
Scale Studies for Jazz Guitar · pp.8–9
Slow → comfortable
Clean · no fx
  • Melodic minor: ascending form uses raised 6th and 7th, descending reverts to natural minor (or stay ascending both ways in jazz context).
  • Run each position on the page two or three times before moving on — don't rush through every position.
  • Even pick attack. If legato, let the hammer-ons and pull-offs speak equally with the picked notes.
  • Apply one position to a short phrase — don't just run scales, end with something musical.

Block 2 · 12 min
Ted Greene — Chord Progressions
Modern Chord Progressions · pp.18–19
Very slow
Clean · let ring
  • First pass: identify each chord symbol. Don't play yet — just read the voicings on the page and find them on the neck.
  • Play each chord and let it ring fully. Listen for the colour of each voicing.
  • Play through the progression slowly. Focus on smooth voice leading — which notes move the least between chords?
  • Pick one voicing that sounds interesting and memorise it. Just one.

Block 4 · 8 min
Rick Stone — Diminished + Whole-Tone
Scale Studies for Jazz Guitar · pp.14–15
Slow · one position at a time
Clean · no fx
  • Diminished (p.14): symmetrical scale — repeats every minor 3rd. Learn one position fully, then notice how the same shape moves up 3 frets and sounds identical.
  • Whole-tone (p.15): all whole steps, very floaty. Six notes only.
  • Don't try to memorise both in one session — pick whichever feels more interesting and stay with it.
  • End with a short phrase using the scale over an open-string drone.

Block 5 · 8 min — Free
Improv — No Score
Connect the day's material
  • Put the phone down. No score.
  • Pick one Ted Greene voicing from today and hold it as a tonal center. Let it ring. Solo over it using any scale from today.
  • Melodic minor over a minor chord. Diminished or whole-tone if you want outside. Don't force it.
  • This isn't about playing well — it's about using what you worked on. Even one phrase that makes sense is a win.
