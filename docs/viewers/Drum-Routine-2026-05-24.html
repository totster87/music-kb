Block 5 · 5 min — Burnout
Trading Bursts
  • 4 bars medium swing → 4 bars full doubletime. Alternate continuously.
  • The swing feel has to stay intact when you drop back — don't let the doubletime blast erase the groove.
  • No click. Push the doubletime harder each cycle. 5 min.
