Warm-Up
01Legato Warm-Up — No Pick 4 min
Technique · No click · No pick
  • Hammer-ons and pull-offs only — pick stays on the table
  • Chromatic 1-2-3-4 across all strings, both directions
  • Left hand has to do all the work — listen for even volume on every note
  • Different warm-up than last session: this isolates left-hand strength and independence from the pick
02Tremolo Burst Drill 3 min
Technique · No click · Right-hand focus
  • 4-note bursts at maximum speed — then full stop. Not sustained tremolo
  • Goal: the burst should feel effortless, not forced. Tension = slower ceiling
  • Try both: all downstrokes burst, then alternate picking burst. Feel the difference
  • Directly relevant to ABR TOAL string-skipping speed
03String-Skip — Lighter Grip Experiment 4 min
Technique · 60–80 BPM
  • Same skip pairs as before — but deliberately loosen pick grip by 30%
  • Last session noted pick attack inconsistency on ABR. A lighter grip often fixes both: fewer clipped strings, more consistent tone
  • If it feels slippery: that's normal. Let the pick find its own angle
Test: Play one pass with your normal grip, then one pass lighter. Note whether the skips clean up — this is the diagnostic from last session applied to warm-up.
