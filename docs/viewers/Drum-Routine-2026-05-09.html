Warm-Up
01Stick Control p.10 — Triplet Combinations 8 min
George Lawrence Stone · Stick Control · p.10 · 60–90 BPM
  • Triplet groupings — a different feel to the eighth-note combos on p.5
  • Start slow: every stroke deliberate, no sloppy rebounds
  • Orchestrate around the kit once the pattern is comfortable on the pad
  • Add 4-on-the-floor and 2+4 hat foot once both hands are locked in
Note: Last time on Stick Control (05/04) was p.5. Triplets sit differently in the body — let the groove settle before adding kit orchestration.

Funk
02Latham — Advanced Funk Studies p.20 10 min
Rick Latham · Advanced Funk Studies · p.20 · 75–100 BPM
  • Deep into the Funk Patterns chapter — syncopated bass drum, layered ghost notes
  • Read the pattern on the snare first before adding bass drum and hi-hat
  • Ghost notes are part of the groove — don't treat them as optional
  • Play each pattern for a full minute before moving to the next
First time with Latham. His notation is dense — read each bar carefully before playing. The written tempo is usually conservative; these feel good at 90–100 BPM once they're under your hands.
Advanced Funk Studies, p.20
Double Bass
03Donati — Double Bass Freedom p.40 10 min
Virgil Donati · Double Bass Freedom · p.40 · 60–85 BPM
  • Developing Single Strokes chapter — alternating strokes between feet, building clarity and evenness
  • Different territory to p.21 (hand-foot independence) and p.59 (sextuplets) from last sessions
  • Play each line 4 times before moving on — no rushing
  • Focus: both feet hitting at equal volume and depth — not dominant foot coasting
Left foot note: At 05/04 session the left foot was unstable above 72 BPM and tip-toed at faster tempos. Single stroke work at this page range is exactly the fix — slow deliberate strokes, not speed.
Double Bass Drum Freedom, p.40
Independence
04Chapin — Advanced Techniques p.25 8 min
Jim Chapin · Advanced Techniques Vol.1 · p.25 · Slow–medium
  • Triplet independence section — ride in triplets, limb independence underneath
  • Different feel to p.18 (dotted eighths) done on 05/05

Jazz / Bop
05Riley — Art of Bop Drumming p.28 8 min
John Riley · Art of Bop Drumming · p.28 · Slow
  • Comping section — mid-range of the chapter, more rhythmic variation than the intro pages
  • Different page to p.22 (05/05) — new comping figures, don't expect them to feel familiar
  • Read the notation fully before picking up sticks — Riley's shorthand can bite you mid-bar
  • Ride must stay steady throughout regardless of what the other limbs do
From 05/05: Ride got shaky at p.22, had to slow down significantly. Keep it slow again here. The ride being rock-solid is the only goal — comping is secondary.

Burnout Finisher
06Sextuplet Sprint 5 min max
No click · Both feet · Push until it blurs
  • Groups of 6 alternating between feet — R L R L R L, continuous
  • Simple hand groove on top (ride + snare on 2+4)
  • Start at a comfortable tempo, push by feel every minute
  • Stop when the sextuplet grouping stops being recognisable — not before
Last burnout (05/06) was straight 16ths. Sextuplet sprint is a different demand — the grouping of 6 keeps the feet honest even at high speed. Push hard.
