Drum Routine

Monday 4 May 2026  ·  ~55 min
Warm-up 10 min Table of Time 10 min Independence 15 min Double bass 15 min Cool-down 5 min
1 — Warm-Up: Stick Control 10 min
Stick Control · Stone · p.5
  • Single Beat Combinations p.5 — rows 1–8
  • Start at 60 BPM 16th notes, push to 80 BPM once relaxed
  • Even accent on beat 1 of each group — feel the pulse, don't just count
  • No wrist tension — this is blood flow, not practice
Open book: Stick Control p.5
2 — Table of Time 10 min
Master Studies · Morello · p.8 · Baseline: 53 BPM halftime
  • Start at 53 BPM halftime — confirm the baseline before pushing
  • Work each subdivision change deliberately — don't let the metronome drag you through
  • At each transition: stop, count the new value out loud × 1 bar, then play
  • If a transition rushes, loop just that crossover — isolate the problem point
  • Try pushing to 55 BPM on any subdivision that feels locked
Known issue: Rushing at subdivision transitions. The fix isn't slowing down after the rush — it's hearing the new value clearly before committing to it.
Open book: Master Studies p.8 — Table of Time
3 — Independence: PI Rock Part 3 15 min
Progressive Independence: Rock · Spagnardi · p.9
  • Exercises 1–4 — same as last session, going deeper not further
  • Hi-hat foot on 2 and 4 throughout
  • Target: each exercise clean × 4 bars at 60 BPM before moving on
  • If an exercise was solid last time, push it to 70 BPM today
  • If any exercise feels shaky, slow to 50 BPM — don't practise the mistake
Key principle: Independence means the limbs don't interfere with each other. If thinking about one limb disrupts another, the tempo is too fast.
Open book: Progressive Independence Rock p.9 — Part 3
4 — Double Bass: Morello Groups of Four 15 min
Master Studies · Morello · p.9 · Baseline: 70 BPM
  • Warm in at 65 BPM — a few minutes before jumping to 70
  • Confirm 70 BPM is still clean — left foot staying even with right
  • If 70 is solid, try 72 BPM for one run — note if the weaker foot ghosts
  • Short bursts (8–16 bars) with rest between — endurance comes later
  • Keep hands playing a simple groove on top — don't drop the upper body while focusing on feet
Watch for: Left foot volume dropping after the first few beats of each group. If it does, back off the tempo — stamina at 65 BPM beats sloppy at 70.
Open book: Master Studies p.9 — Groups of Four
5 — Cool-Down 5 min
Free play
  • Free groove — no click, no structure
  • Easy tempo, light touch on everything
  • Let the feet rest
After session: What BPM did Groups of Four feel comfortable at? Did Table of Time transitions feel cleaner? Any PI Rock exercises that clicked? Log it.
