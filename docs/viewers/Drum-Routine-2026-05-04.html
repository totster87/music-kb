Drum Routine

Monday 4 May 2026  ·  ~65 min
Warm-up 10 min Table of Time 10 min Independence 15 min Double bass 15 min You Got Me 10 min Cool-down 5 min
1 — Warm-Up: Stick Control 10 min
Stick Control · Stone · p.5
  • Single Beat Combinations — rows 1–8
  • Start at 60 BPM 16th notes, push to 80 BPM once relaxed
  • Even accent on beat 1 of each group — feel the pulse
  • No wrist tension — this is blood flow, not a test
Stick Control — p.5
2 — Table of Time 10 min
Master Studies · Morello · p.8 · Baseline: 53 BPM halftime
  • Start at 53 BPM halftime — confirm the baseline before pushing
  • At each subdivision change: stop, count the new value out loud × 1 bar, then play
  • If a transition rushes, loop just that crossover point
  • Try 55 BPM on any subdivision that feels locked in
Known issue: Rushing at subdivision transitions. Hear the new value clearly before committing to it — count out loud first.
Master Studies — Table of Time, p.8
3 — Independence: PI Rock Part 3 15 min
Progressive Independence: Rock · Spagnardi · p.9
  • Exercises 1–4 — going deeper, not further
  • Hi-hat foot on 2 and 4 throughout
  • Each exercise clean × 4 bars at 60 BPM before moving on
  • Any exercise solid from last time → push to 70 BPM
  • Any exercise shaky → drop to 50 BPM, don't practise the mistake
Key principle: If thinking about one limb disrupts another, the tempo is too fast.
PI Rock — Part 3, p.9
4 — Double Bass: Morello Groups of Four 15 min
Master Studies · Morello · p.9 · Baseline: 70 BPM
  • Warm in at 65 BPM for a few minutes before jumping to 70
  • Confirm 70 BPM is still clean — left foot staying even with right
  • If 70 is solid, try 72 BPM — back off if the weaker foot ghosts
  • Short bursts (8–16 bars) with rest between — endurance comes later
  • Keep a simple hand groove on top — don't drop the upper body while focusing on feet
Watch for: Left foot volume dropping after the first few beats. Stamina at 65 beats sloppy at 70.
Master Studies — Groups of Four, p.9
5 — Music Application: You Got Me 10 min
The Roots · Transcription · p.1
  • Read through the transcription — feel the pocket before worrying about accuracy
  • No click to start — play along to the record to lock into the original feel
  • Note any bars or fills that need isolating for next session
You Got Me — p.1
6 — Cool-Down 5 min
Free play
  • Free groove — no click, no structure
  • Easy tempo, light touch on everything
  • Let the feet rest
After session: What BPM did Groups of Four feel comfortable at? Did Table of Time transitions feel cleaner? Any PI Rock exercises that clicked? How did You Got Me feel? Log it.
