Warm-Up
01Chromatic 1-2-3-4 3 min
Technique · No click
  • Start low E, one finger per fret, ascend to high E and back
  • Every finger fully down before the next lifts — no ghost notes
  • No metronome — just get the hands warm and connected
02Spider Walk 3 min
Technique · All strings
  • 1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-1, 3-4-1-2, 4-1-2-3 — rotate the starting finger
  • Move across all six strings, both directions
  • Focus on right-hand accuracy — land on the right string every time
03String-Skip Isolation Drill 5 min
Technique · 50–70 BPM
  • Skip one string — low E to D, A to G, D to B, G to high E
  • Then skip two strings — low E to G, A to B
  • Alternate picking only — don't let the skip turn into a sweep
  • Last session: pick noise + fretting buzz — focus on clean landings this time
Key: The pick doesn't need to go wide — tighten the arc. Big motion = clipped strings. Small controlled motion = clean skip.
04Bend Accuracy Check 3 min
Technique · No click
  • Play a reference note, then bend to match it from a whole step below
  • Try all strings, multiple fret positions
  • Three fingers supporting every bend — don't single-finger it
  • Relevance: Treatment pinch harmonics + Better Lovers Section B bends

  • Work up from ♩=60 to ♩=100 once it's clean

08Section B — Bar 23 Skip + Bars 24–25 Bend 5 min
Better Lovers · p.9 · Two weak spots, one block
  • Bar 23: the downward chord skip — the one that keeps getting missed. Slow, deliberate, commit to the skip direction
  • Bars 24–25: follow through on the bend fully — pick hits the string, bend commits, don't abort mid-bend
  • Link bars 23–25 as a unit once each feels solid
Common cause: Both problems are hesitation. The pick knows where to go but the body second-guesses it. Trust the motion.

ABR — Truth of a Liar
10Section A — Single-String Tremolo Speed Check 4 min
ABR Truth of a Liar · Technique diagnosis
  • Tremolo pick a single string at max controlled speed — what BPM do you cap out?
  • Now do the same on a string-skip pair — what's the cap there?
  • The gap between those two numbers is the actual string-skip deficit
  • This is diagnostic — no tempo target, just measure the gap
Context: Last session (05/03) single-string was noticeably faster than string-skipping. Quantify it today so you can track whether it's closing.
ABR — Truth of a Liar (Guitar), p.7
11Section A — String Skip Pairs in Isolation 5 min
ABR Truth of a Liar · Weak spot drill

  • Not the whole riff — just the two-note skip motion, over and over
  • 80 BPM to start, push until it breaks, back off one notch
  • Goal: make the skip motion feel as automatic as single-string picking

12Section A — BPM Automation 6 min
ABR Truth of a Liar · Max: 120 BPM · Target: 125+

  • Increment by 2 BPM every clean minute
  • Last session: 105 iffy, 118 sloppy, 120 max — try to push that ceiling today
  • Note exact BPM where it breaks and why (string skip? pick attack? both?)
Pick attack note: Still unresolved from last session — unclear how hard to hit the strings. Try a lighter attack in this session and see if the skips clean up.

ABR — Treatment
13Pinch Harmonic Drill — Angle Finding 5 min
ABR Treatment · Drop C · Pinch harmonic isolation
  • No tempo — just find a pick angle + squeeze combination that fires the harmonic reliably
  • Try: more pick exposed / less pick exposed / edge of pick / flat of pick
  • Try: picking nearer the bridge vs. the neck pickup
  • Once you find one that works 8/10 times — lock in that position and don't change it
Known issue: Frequently missed on Treatment Section A. Root cause is inconsistent pick angle and squeeze timing. Fix the angle first, then the timing will follow.

BTBAM — Prequel to the Sequel
16Intro Lead — Descending Runs Only 5 min
BTBAM · Standard tuning · Known weak spot
  • Extract every descending run from the intro lead and loop each one
  • The problem: pick misses the target string even at slow tempos — this is right-hand accuracy, not speed
  • Start at ♩=50 — slow enough to feel the pick land on the correct string each time
  • No ascending runs this block — ascending is fine. Descending only.
Tip: Watch the picking hand. On descending runs many players unconsciously sweep rather than alternate pick. If that's happening, exaggerate the upstroke to force the alternation.

Free Play
20Open — Play Something For Fun 5 min
No rules · No click · No goals
  • Play whatever sounds good — a song, a riff, improvisation, whatever
  • No technique goals, no BPM targets, no correction
  • End the session on enjoyment — not the last hard thing you were trying to fix
