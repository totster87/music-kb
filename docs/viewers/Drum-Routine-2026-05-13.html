Drum Routine

Wednesday 13 May 2026  ·  Converge — Black Cloud  ·  Learn + Record Reel
Single song focus. No books today — all time goes to Black Cloud. Goal: learn the full track and record a one-take reel by end of session.
Warm-Up
01Aggression Warm-Up 5 min
Pad + kit · No click · Full intensity
  • Fast alternating single strokes on pad — push to near-max tempo, stay loose
  • Then move to kit: ride + snare accents, build up the attack in both hands
  • Bass drum bursts — 16ths at high tempo, both feet alternating, 30 sec on / 15 sec off × 3
  • Don't hold back — this session needs full energy from the start
Context: Koller plays with controlled violence — technically precise but physically aggressive. Warm up to match that energy, not to ease into it.
Song Map
02Full Listen — Map the Structure 5 min
Converge · Black Cloud · Axe to Fall (2009) · Ben Koller
  • One full listen with no kit — just ears and a mental map
  • Identify: how many distinct sections, where the big tempo or feel changes are
  • Mark the hardest moment in the song — that’s where the session time goes
  • Note any syncopated figures, irregular bar lengths, or rhythmic traps before touching the kit
▶ https://youtu.be/xPX6DLQZ2T4?si=pzBhhTJXk_Hc93mP
Koller’s approach: He plays with enormous dynamic range — not everything is at max. Listen for where he pulls back and where he pushes. The contrast is part of the song.
Section Learning
03Section by Section — Build the Map 15 min
Learn by ear · Loop sections · No rushing ahead
  • Start from the top. Loop the intro until it’s solid, then move forward
  • Each section: listen once, play once with recording, then play without
  • When you hit a wall — loop that moment at half speed, isolate the specific coordination
  • Don’t run the full song until you’ve mapped every section at least once
  • The goal here is coverage — getting through every part of the track, even roughly
Trap to avoid: Don’t spend all 15 minutes on the intro. Even if it’s not clean, keep moving forward so you know the full shape of the song before drilling.
Problem Spots
04Isolate the Hard Bit 10 min
Slow loop · 2–4 bars max · Targeted
  • Pick the one or two moments from the song map that felt impossible — work only those
  • Isolate to 2–4 bars. Loop them at 60–70% tempo until the coordination is automatic
  • Bring it up to full tempo only when it’s clean at slow speed — not before
  • If a fill or transition is the problem: strip it to just hands, then add feet, then add speed
Koller specifics to watch for: Syncopated bass drum figures under fast hands, unexpected bar length shifts, snare backbeats that land off the grid. These are the usual culprits in his writing.
Full Run-Throughs
05Full Song — 3 Passes 10 min
Full tempo · With recording · Don’t stop
  • Play the full track start to finish three times, with the recording playing
  • Do not stop for mistakes — lock back in and keep going. Stopping trains stopping
  • After each pass: note one thing to fix, one thing that felt good. Don’t overanalyse
  • By pass 3 you should have a working version of the whole song, even if rough
Mindset shift: You’re not trying to play it perfectly — you’re trying to play it all the way through. The reel is about energy and commitment, not perfection.
Reel Recording
06Record the Reel 5 min
One take · No editing · Full send
  • Set up the camera before you start — phone on a stand, framing the kit
  • One take. Start the song, play the full thing, stop when it ends
  • Don’t re-record because of mistakes — the reel is a document of where you’re at today
  • If you want a second take, do it — but post the most energetic version, not the cleanest
