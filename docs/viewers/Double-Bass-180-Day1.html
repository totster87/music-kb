Block 2 · 15 min — Today's Focus
Donati Ch.2 — Left Foot Technique
pp.28–30 · fix the startup flutter
Slow — 80–120 BPM
Left foot entry
  • The problem: note 1 lands fine, but strokes 2 and 3 flutter before the left foot settles into the groove. The foot is cold-starting — needs a couple of strokes to ramp up to speed.
  • The fix — pre-activation: before launching a run, lightly tap the left foot on the pedal 2–3 times at the target tempo. Get it moving before note 1, not after. The foot should already be in motion when the run starts.
  • Restart drill: RLRL × 4 notes, hard stop, immediately restart without hesitation. The restart moment is exactly where strokes 2–3 go wrong — drill it until the re-entry is as clean as the middle of a run.
  • At slow tempo: consciously place stroke 2 and stroke 3 with intention. Slow enough to think about each left foot hit individually before it becomes automatic.
  • Slow is fine — 80 BPM with strokes 2–3 clean beats 160 BPM with early-run flutter.

Block 4 · 5 min — Burnout (every session)
Straight 16th Endurance
  • Set click to today's ceiling BPM. Play straight alternating 16ths continuously.
  • Hold as long as form stays clean. When it breaks, stop — rest 30 seconds — go again.
  • 3 rounds. This is conditioning, not technique work — push through discomfort.
  • Log how long you held each round (seconds). This number should grow across the week.
