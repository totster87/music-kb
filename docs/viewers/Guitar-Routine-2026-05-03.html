Guitar Routine — 2026-05-03

Drop D · ~55 min · String skipping · Palm muting · New song exposure

Warm-Up — 10 min

Chromatic spider runs across all 6 strings. 60 BPM 16th notes, push to 80. Even, light pick attack throughout.

Then: open string arpeggios in Drop D — D major, D minor, D sus2. Let ring. Settles the picking hand before real work.

Section 1 — String Skipping, Clean Tone — 15 min

Better Lovers — Lie Between the Lines, Clean Intro
Isolate every string-skip: open low D jumping to B or e string. Slow → 60 → 75 → 99 BPM. Wrist lifts slightly after low D to clear the G string.

Work 2-bar chunks. 4 clean passes before moving on. No G string noise on any skip.

Section 2 — Palm Muting Consistency — 15 min

Better Lovers — Lie Between the Lines, Section B (♩=135)
Heavy palm mute on open D. Every hit identical — muted, tight, no variation. 100 BPM → 120 → 135. If mute lifts under speed, slow back down.

Palm sits just behind the bridge. Any further back = too muddy. Right hand consistency is the whole game here.

Section 3 — New Song Exposure — 10 min

Better Lovers — Your Misplaced Self, opening (p.20)
First page of the next song. No tempo target — just read slowly, find the patterns, note anything that looks tricky for next session.

Goal is familiarity, not performance. Mark any bar that looks like a problem.

Cool-Down — 5 min

Free playing in Drop D. No structure, no goal. Let the hands relax.
