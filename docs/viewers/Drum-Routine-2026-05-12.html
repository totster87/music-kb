01Reed — Syncopation p.28 7 min
Ted Reed · Syncopation · p.28 · 60–80 BPM
  • Syncopated Eighth Notes chapter — new section, different character to p.37/41 done before
  • Read it on snare only first — don't touch the kit until the reading is solid
  • Then orchestrate: melody hand reads the line, bass drum and hat foot hold time
  • Keep tempo slow enough that you're reading ahead, not reacting

Control Studies
02Morello — Stone Killer p.54 10 min
Joe Morello · Master Studies · p.54 · Slow → push
  • Stone Killer — Control Studies chapter, different beast to Table of Time (p.43) or the fill-in studies
  • Read the exercise title literally: it is designed to be uncomfortable. That’s the point
  • Start well below tempo — accuracy first, speed second
  • Orchestrate around the kit once the sticking pattern is locked on pad
Morello history: p.43 (Table of Time), p.71 (Groups of Four), p.30 (Stroke Combos). Stone Killer is the other landmark in the Control Studies chapter — harder than Table of Time.
Master Studies, p.54
Independence
03PI Rock p.84 — Section 4, 16th Note Ride 8 min
Ron Spagnardi · Progressive Independence Rock · p.84 · 65–85 BPM
  • Section 4 — ride hand plays continuous 16th notes. Completely different feel to S1/S2/S3
  • 16ths on the ride is demanding: right hand locks in first, independence underneath second
  • Don’t let the ride hand slow down when the other limbs get busy — it’s the anchor
  • Start at a tempo where the 16th note ride feels effortless before adding the bass/snare patterns
PI Rock progression: p.9 (S1 eighths) → p.45 (S2 quarters) → p.70 (S3 upbeats) → p.84 (S4 sixteenths). Each section adds a new right-hand challenge — this is the most physically demanding yet.
Progressive Independence Rock, p.84
Burnout Finisher
04Trading Bursts 5 min
No click · Swing → full tempo · Alternate
  • 4 bars medium swing tempo — relaxed, ride-based, real feel
  • 4 bars full doubletime — push everything, hold the swing phrasing as long as possible
  • Alternate continuously. The recovery bars (medium swing) are not rest — keep the groove
  • Stop when the doubletime completely loses its swing shape
Rotation: Straight 16ths → Sextuplet sprint → Kick+Snare Unison → Open sprint → Doubletime Bop (05/11) → Trading Bursts today.
