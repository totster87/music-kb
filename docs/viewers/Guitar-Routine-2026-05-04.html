5 — ABR: Truth of a Liar 15 min
After the Burial · Section A · Drop D · Max: 120 BPM · Target: 130
  • BPM automation from 75 BPM, +1 BPM increments
  • Last session max was 120 — aim to push into the 121–125 range today

6 — Pinch Harmonic Isolation 5 min
Technique · For ABR Treatment
  • Drop C tuning — open low C string, palm mute position
  • Hit single pinch harmonic repeatedly — find the exact pick angle and thumb contact point that triggers it cleanly
  • Slow, deliberate — one hit at a time. Don't rush to tempo
  • Once you find the sweet spot: lock in that grip before moving to the full riff
Fix cue: The thumb edge needs to graze the string immediately after the pick hits. Angle the pick slightly inward and squeeze through — if you're missing, the pick is too vertical or the thumb contact is too late.
7 — ABR: Treatment 15 min
After the Burial · Section A · Drop C · Guitar 1 · Max: ~130 BPM · Target: 195
  • Tune down to Drop C (C G C F A D) before starting
  • Open at 100 BPM — confirm the pattern is under fingers before pushing
  • Work toward 130 BPM — that was last session's ceiling
  • Pinch harmonics: use the grip from block 6. Don't skip past the misses — stop and reset
  • Try one pass at 135 BPM near the end if 130 feels clean
Known issue: Pinch harmonics missed frequently above 130. Priority is accuracy, not speed — a missed PH at 135 is worse than a clean one at 125.
8 — BTBAM: Prequel to the Sequel 10 min
Between the Buried and Me · Intro lead melody · Standard · Max: 90 BPM · Target: 100
  • Single note lines only — lead melody, no chords
  • Start at 70 BPM — slow enough to get pick placement right before building speed
  • On every descending run: watch where the pick lands. Slow it down the moment it clips
  • Build to 90 BPM — last session's max. Try 92 BPM if 90 feels stable
  • Quality over tempo — one clean run at 88 is worth more than five sloppy ones at 92
Known issue: Pick accuracy on descending runs — clips wrong string even at slow tempos. This is a motion problem, not a speed problem. Slow down until every note lands clean, then build.
