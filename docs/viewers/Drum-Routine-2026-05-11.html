Burnout Finisher
05Doubletime Bop 5 min max
No click · Swing feel · Push the double
  • Pick a medium swing tempo — somewhere around 120 BPM quarter note feels comfortable
  • Play it straight for 4 bars, then double the tempo and hold the ride swing feel
  • The doubletime ride should still feel relaxed, not frantic — it's swing at speed, not blast
  • Alternate: 4 bars medium / 4 bars doubletime. Keep going until the doubletime falls apart
