1 — Chapin: Four-Way Coordination 15 min
Advanced Techniques for the Modern Drummer · Chapin · p.18
  • Read through the page before playing — know what each limb is doing
  • Start at 60 BPM — Chapin patterns collapse fast if you go in too hot
  • Isolate any exercise where a limb drops out under pressure — that's the one to loop
  • Hi-hat foot active throughout — don't let it go silent when hands get busy
  • Push to 70 BPM only when the page feels like four independent voices, not a juggling act
Goal: True independence — each limb unaware of the others. If thinking about the kick disrupts the ride, the tempo is too fast.
Advanced Techniques — p.18
2 — Riley: Bop Comping 10 min
The Art of Bop Drumming · Riley · p.22
  • Ride cymbal steady — this is the foundation, not optional
  • Comping figures are suggestions, not rules — feel free to mix and match across the page
  • Hi-hat on 2 and 4, keep it locked regardless of what the hands do
  • No click — the ride is the click. If the ride wavers, slow down
  • Aim for relaxed swing feel — stiff bop is no bop at all
Mindset: The snare and kick are punctuation. The ride and hi-hat are the sentence. Don't let the punctuation drive the tempo.
Art of Bop Drumming — p.22
3 — Donati: Sextuplet Series 15 min
Double Bass Drum Freedom · Donati · p.59
  • Read the page first — sextuplets have a specific feel, hear it in your head before touching the pedals
  • Start slow: 50 BPM with the sextuplet subdivision feeling like triplet pairs
  • Even stroke weight across all six notes — the middle notes are where evenness dies
  • Short bursts only — 4 bars on, rest, repeat. Sextuplets burn out fast
  • If the pattern starts to blur into a roll, you've lost the subdivision — stop and reset
Watch for: Notes 3 and 4 in each group getting squashed — the turnaround point between feet is where evenness breaks down. Slow it until every note has the same weight and space.
Double Bass Drum Freedom — p.59
4 — Music Application: You Got Me 10 min
The Roots · Transcription · p.1 · Last: 83 BPM
  • Play along to the record — no click, lock into the original feel
  • After the Chapin and Donati work, this should feel like a release — let it breathe
  • Try one pass with eyes closed: feel the pocket, don't read it
  • Note any fills that still feel shaky — those are next session's isolation targets
Focus: Internal clock and feel. The groove is the challenge, not the difficulty of the notes.
You Got Me — p.1
