5 · Groups of Four — Double Bass Kit 15 min
Master Studies — Joe Morello · p.9
♩= 70 BPM · Last session baseline. Push carefully — don't sacrifice evenness for speed
  • Start at 65 BPM — get a feel for both feet before adding speed
  • 70 BPM: the target. Focus on even weight between left and right foot — left tends to drag

  • Rest for 30 seconds between each variation — this is taxing, don't white-knuckle through fatigue
  • Note your top clean BPM — log it after the session
