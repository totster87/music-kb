Pad Routine

Wednesday 6 May 2026  ·  Practice pad only  ·  ~40 min
Stick Control 10 min Morello Accents 10 min Reed Reading 10 min Rudiment block 5 min Burnout 5 min
1 — Stick Control: Single Beats 10 min
Stick Control · Stone · p.5
  • Rows 1–8 — both hands even, no favourites
  • Start at 60 BPM 16th notes — loose wrists, no gripping
  • Listen for volume balance between hands — weaker hand tends to drop
  • Push to 100 BPM gradually — back off the moment tension creeps in
  • Try accenting different beats: beat 1, then beat 3, then every other note
Stick Control — p.5
2 — Morello: Accent Studies 10 min
Master Studies · Morello · p.7
  • Accented notes full stroke, unaccented notes low — exaggerate the difference at first
  • Start at 60 BPM — the accent control is the point, not the speed
  • Both hands: don't let the strong hand carry the accents while the weak hand ghosts everything
  • Push to 80 BPM once the dynamic contrast is consistent
Key principle: The unaccented notes should be almost inaudible against the accented ones. If everything sounds the same volume, the accents aren't working.
Master Studies — Accent Studies, p.7
3 — Reed: Syncopation Reading 10 min
Progressive Steps to Syncopation · Reed · p.38
  • Read on the snare only — no orchestration today, just accurate reading
  • Count out loud while playing — especially through rests, don't let them rush
  • Start at 60 BPM, stay there until the line feels natural
  • If a bar rushes, loop it. Don't move forward on a mistake
  • Goal: one full page clean at a steady tempo
Lesson from last session: Stay on snare reading for several bars before going anywhere near the kit. The reading has to be solid first.
Progressive Steps to Syncopation — p.38
4 — Rudiment Block 5 min
Free choice · Paradiddles / Doubles / 5-stroke roll
  • Pick two or three rudiments — whichever feel weakest lately
  • Paradiddle: RLRR LRLL — accent the first note of each group
  • Double stroke roll: push for evenness on the second stroke, that's where it blurs
  • 5-stroke roll: feel the accent landing on the beat, not floating off it
  • No click — just hands, feel, and control
Mindset: Rudiments are maintenance. Pick the ones that feel the least automatic and spend the time there.
5 — Burnout: Single Stroke Sprint 5 min
No click · Max speed · Hold until it blurs
  • Single stroke roll — build from comfortable to absolute max over 30 seconds
  • Hold at max until the strokes start to blur or uneven out, then stop
  • Rest 20 seconds, go again — 3 rounds
  • Don't tense up chasing speed — if the grip tightens, back off
After session: How was the volume balance between hands on Stick Control? Did the Reed reading stay on snare this time? What rudiments felt weakest? Log it.
