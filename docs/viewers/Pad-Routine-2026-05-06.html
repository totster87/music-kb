Pad Routine

1 — Stick Control: Accent Combinations 10 min
Stick Control · Stone · p.14
  • Further into the book than usual — read through the page before playing
  • Accented notes are full strokes from high up; unaccented are tap strokes, staying low
  • Start at 60 BPM — control first, speed never
  • Listen for the dynamic contrast: the gap between loud and soft should be obvious
  • Work to 90 BPM once the accents are consistent
Watch for: Unaccented notes creeping up in volume as tempo rises. The moment they equalise, you've lost the exercise.
Stick Control — p.14
2 — 20 Chops 10 min
20 Chops · pp.2–3
  • Read through both pages — these are hand chops, all pad-applicable
  • Pick two exercises and go deep on each — don't skim the whole page
  • No click to start — feel the sticking pattern before locking to a tempo
  • Add click once the pattern is automatic — then push it
  • Note which chops feel weakest — those are the ones worth returning to
Mindset: Chops are vocabulary. The goal isn't just to execute them — it's to own them at speed without thinking.
20 Chops — p.2
20 Chops — p.3
3 — Chapin: Hand Patterns 10 min
Advanced Techniques for the Modern Drummer · Chapin · p.25
  • Hands only — ignore any foot notation for now, this is pure hand coordination on the pad
  • Read through before playing — Chapin patterns need to be understood before they can be felt
  • Start at 60 BPM — these patterns collapse fast if you go in hot
  • Try the metronome on 2 and 4 only rather than every beat — forces you to feel the pulse internally
  • Push to 75 BPM once the hands are truly independent of the counting
Key principle: If you're still consciously thinking about what each hand is doing, the tempo is too fast. Slow down until it becomes automatic.
Advanced Techniques — p.25
4 — Reed: Syncopation Reading 10 min
Progressive Steps to Syncopation · Reed · p.38
  • Snare only — no orchestration, just reading
  • Count out loud through every rest — rests are where rushing happens
  • Stay on one line until it's clean before moving to the next
  • Goal: one full page at a steady 70 BPM with no hesitations
Lesson carried forward: Stay on snare reading for the full block. Don't touch the kit until the reading is solid — that's the whole point of today.
Progressive Steps to Syncopation — p.38
5 — Burnout: Single Stroke Sprint 5 min
No click · Max speed · 3 rounds
  • Single stroke roll — build to absolute max over 20–30 seconds
  • Hold at max until strokes blur or become uneven, then stop
  • Rest 20 seconds, repeat — 3 rounds total
  • Don't grip — speed from relaxation, not tension
After session: Which chops from 20 Chops felt weakest? Did the Reed reading stay on snare the whole time? What BPM did Chapin p.25 start to fall apart? Log it.
