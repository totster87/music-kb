1 — Stick Control: Accents, Doubles, Flams 15 min
Stick Control · Stone · pp.14, 22, 30
  • Three pages, five minutes each — read the page before playing it
  • p.14 — Accent combos: full strokes on accents, tap strokes on everything else. Dynamic gap should be obvious
  • p.22 — Double beats: second stroke of each pair must match the first in volume and depth — that's where doubles fall apart
  • p.30 — Flam studies: grace note lands just before the beat, not on it — if they sound simultaneous, the grace note is too late
  • Start each page at 60 BPM, push to 90 BPM once the technique is clean
Watch for: Each page tests something different. Don't let the muscle memory from one page bleed into the next — reset between pages.

2 — 20 Chops 10 min
20 Chops · pp.2–4
  • Pick two or three chops and go deep — don't skim all three pages
  • No click first — feel the sticking pattern, then add tempo
  • Push each chop to the edge of clean, note where it breaks
Mindset: Own two chops completely rather than touching ten superficially.

3 — Chapin: Hand Patterns 10 min
Advanced Techniques for the Modern Drummer · Chapin · p.25
  • Hands only on the pad — ignore foot notation
  • Metronome on 2 and 4 only — feel the pulse internally between clicks
  • Start at 60 BPM, push to 75 BPM once automatic
  • If counting out loud helps, do it — Chapin patterns need to be internalised before they can be felt
Key principle: Still thinking about what each hand is doing means the tempo is too fast.

4 — Reed: Syncopation Reading 10 min
Progressive Steps to Syncopation · Reed · pp.38, 44, 52
  • Snare only — all three pages, no orchestration
  • Count out loud through every rest — don't let them rush
  • Spend 3–4 minutes per page: one clean pass at 60 BPM, then push
  • If a bar trips you up, loop it until it's boring, then move on
Carry forward: Reading stays on snare the whole block. That's the discipline — not the tempo.

5 — Burnout: Single Stroke Sprint 5 min
No click · Max speed · 3 rounds
  • Single stroke roll — build to absolute max over 20–30 seconds
  • Hold until strokes blur or become uneven, then stop
  • Rest 20 seconds, repeat — 3 rounds total
  • Speed from relaxation, not tension — if the grip tightens, back off
After session: Which Stick Control page was hardest? Which chops from 20 Chops felt weakest? Did the Reed reading stay on snare? Log it.
