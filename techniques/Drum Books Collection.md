# Drum Books Collection

## Transcriptions & Style Studies
- August Burns Red — Messengers (drum transcription book)
- Off the Record — David Garibaldi; 10 Tower of Power transcriptions
- Beyond Bop Drumming — covers Elvin Jones, Paul Motian, Ed Blackwell, Tony Williams, Jack DeJohnette

## Technique & Rudiments
- Advanced Techniques for the Modern Drummer
- Master Studies II — Joe Morello
- Accents and Rebounds
- Modern Rudimental Swing Solos — Charley Wilcoxon; for advanced drummer

## Jazz & Melodic Development
- Wayne Salzmann II — Developing Melodic Language on the Drums
- The Sound of Brushes — Ed Thigpen; swing, bebop, punk, R&B brush patterns

## Double Bass
- Double Bass Freedom — Virgil Donati; highly recommended by user

## Independence
- Progressive Independence: Rock — Ron Spagnardi; comprehensive snare/bass drum independence for rock and funk
- Advanced Techniques for the Modern Drummer Vol.1 — Jim Chapin; coordinated independence as applied to jazz and be-bop
- The Art of Bop Drumming — John Riley; jazz comping, ride cymbal, and bop vocabulary

## Technique & Rudiments
- Stick Control for the Snare Drummer — George Lawrence Stone; the foundational sticking pattern bible
- Master Studies — Joe Morello; exercises for development of control and technique
- Progressive Steps to Syncopation for the Modern Drummer — Ted Reed; syncopation and reading

## Funk & Feel
- Advanced Funk Studies — Rick Latham; creative funk patterns in the style of today's leading funk drummers

## Foundational
- Progressive Steps to Syncopation for the Modern Drummer — Ted Reed
