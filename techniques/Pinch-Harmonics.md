# Pinch Harmonics

Producing a harmonic by lightly grazing the thumb on the string immediately after the pick stroke. Produces a high-pitched squeal, common in metal.

## Current State
- Hit rate: ~50% or less even in isolation
- Reliable only on G string (3rd) and above — unreliable on B and low strings
- Working focus: G string first, lock in the mechanics before moving lower

## The Mechanics
- Thumb grazes the string immediately after the pick makes contact
- Pick angle slightly inward, squeeze through the note
- Missing = pick too vertical, or thumb contact too late
- Must be repeatable at tempo — one clean PH at 128 BPM beats a missed one at 133

## Isolation Drill
- Open low C (Drop C tuning), palm muted
- Single pinch harmonic, one hit at a time
- Find the exact pick angle and thumb contact point
- Lock it in before adding tempo or context
