# Drum Session — 2026-05-21

**Blocks completed:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (burnout skipped)

## Block 1 — Morgenstein L2–L3 (pp.8–10)
- 90 BPM
- Scattered approach — worked around the kit rather than staying on snare
- Good physical workout regardless

## Block 2 — Stick Control pp.10–11 (Short Roll Combinations)
- 90 BPM, same feel as block 1
- Good warmup material. Doesn't feel musical played straight — tries to orchestrate around kit to compensate.
- Note: probably more useful if treated as pure sticking drill, not a groove source

## Block 3 — Wilcoxon — Modern Rudimental Swing Solos p.17
- Challenging but not fun on drumset — better suited to pad or snare only
- May skip this in future kit sessions or save for pad-only practice days

## Block 4 — Donati Ch.2 — Left Foot Technique (pp.28–30)
- Fun block — great isolated left foot workout
- Key observation: left foot is late getting on the grid at the start of a note chain. Gets on the beat after a couple of notes, not on the first. Feels fluttery/delayed at entry.
- This is different from the "fluttering once running" issue — it's a *startup* problem

## Block 5 — Latham Advanced Funk Studies (pp.20–21)
- p.20: played slow at 37 BPM per bar — material is dense, needs time
- p.21: the 4+5 pattern breakdown was fun — interesting rhythmic feel

## Block 6 — ABR Messengers Drums — The Blinding Light (pp.39–41)
- First session
- Much slower than expected — tempo wasn't established
- Need to map the form and try individual sections before attempting full tempo
