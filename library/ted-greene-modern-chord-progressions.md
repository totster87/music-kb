## Notes
- Dense chord voicing book — jazz harmony, extended and altered chords
- **Pick technique warning:** Many voicings are extremely stretchy and span 5–6 strings. With a pick, either omit the bass note or play the most important inner voices only. Fingerstyle unlocks the full voicing.
- Best approach: decide at the start of a block whether you're going pick (partial voicing) or fingerstyle (full voicing). Don't fight the pick against a 5-fret stretch.
- Good for: chord vocabulary, voice leading, jazz harmony ear training
